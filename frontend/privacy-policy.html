Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

AliSync is operated by tabtab llc. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website, create an account, or use AliSync to connect Shopify and AliExpress.

Information we collectHow we use itSharing informationBrowser storageRetention and deletionSecurityYour choicesContact us

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, name and contact number when provided, password hash, verification status, plan selection and account preferences. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive the profile information needed to identify your account, such as your email and name.
  • Connected-store information: Shopify store domain, store identifiers, granted permissions, connection status, access tokens and credentials needed for authorized connections.
  • Product and supplier information: product and variant identifiers, titles, descriptions, images, prices, stock quantities, supplier details, variant mappings, pricing rules and synchronization history.
  • AliExpress connection information: authorization tokens and, where supplied, API credentials used to retrieve supplier information.
  • Service records: notifications, errors, support messages and technical information processed by our servers or hosting services, such as request details and IP addresses.

AliSync's current product-import and synchronization features do not import Shopify customers' profiles or order records. Shopify privacy requests may contain customer identifiers; we process these requests without saving their customer payload in the application database.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, connect stores and suppliers, import products, synchronize prices and inventory, map variants, replace supplier links, apply plan access, send account or product notifications, provide support, investigate errors and protect the service.

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we process information to provide the service you request, for legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, to meet legal obligations, or with consent where required.

3. When information is shared

AliSync exchanges product and connection information with Shopify and AliExpress to carry out the actions you authorize. Google processes authentication when you choose Google sign-in. Hosting, database and email service providers process information needed to run the service and deliver messages.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or security, or manage a business transfer subject to appropriate privacy protections. Connected platforms handle information under their own privacy policies.

Service providers and connected platforms may process information outside your country. Applicable transfer safeguards depend on the service provider, destination and legal requirements.

4. Cookies and browser storage

AliSync uses browser session storage to keep your sign-in token and selected-store identifier while you use the app. Clearing this storage or signing out can end your session. Connected services such as Shopify or Google may use their own cookies during authorization. This policy page does not require sign-in and does not load analytics scripts or external fonts.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain account and connected-store information while it is needed to provide the service and maintain your requested settings. Retention also depends on support, security, dispute-resolution and legal requirements. You can request information about retention or ask us to delete your account using the email below.

When we receive and verify Shopify's store-redaction request, AliSync deletes the matching store's local products, mappings, pending stock records, notifications, synchronization logs, plan reservations and stored Shopify connection details. This does not delete products from your Shopify store.

Removing one store does not automatically delete your separate AliSync account, AliExpress connection or other connected stores. Contact us to request deletion of those records. Copies in infrastructure logs or backups, where present, may remain until their retention cycles expire or where retention is legally required.

6. Keeping information secure

We use safeguards including password hashing, encryption for stored integration secrets, access controls and verification of Shopify webhook signatures. Access tokens are sensitive information used to perform authorized tasks. No transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security; please keep your account credentials private.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage available account settings, notification preferences and connected-platform permissions. Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority.

Email kabirggod@gmail.com with your request and the account email or store domain needed to identify the records. Do not send passwords, API secrets or access tokens. We may verify your identity and authority before acting and will respond within applicable legal time limits. Shopify customers can also contact the merchant whose store they used.

8. Children

AliSync is a business tool intended for adult merchants and authorized business users. It is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or our practices change. We will publish the revised policy here with an updated date and provide additional notice where required.
